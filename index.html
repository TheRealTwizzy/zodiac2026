The zodiac, explained properly

An interactive map of a 2,000-year-old symbolic system — twelve signs, ten celestial bodies, twelve houses and the real geometry that ties them together. No horoscopes. No predictions. Just the structure, made clear, plus the actual astronomy underneath it.

The question everyone arrives with

The whole system in one sentence

Every statement in astrology is built from those three parts: a planet (what kind of energy), in a sign (the style it acts with), in a house (the area of life it shows up in). Learn those three and you can read anything the tradition throws at you.

Section three

The Twelve Houses

Signs are slices of the sky. Houses are slices of your day — twelve sectors fixed to the horizon at the moment and place you were born. Same wheel, different question: signs say how, houses say where in life.

Section four

Angles & Aspects

An aspect is nothing more than the angle between two points on a 360° circle. A handful of those angles were singled out as meaningful — and every one of them divides the circle by a small whole number. Drag the two markers and watch the angle name change.

Reference

How to Read This Site

What this is, what it is not, how the numbers are produced, and where they stop being trustworthy. This page exists because a site that computes real sky positions and prints astrological labels next to them owes you a clear account of which is which.

The short version. Where the planets were is a matter of fact, computed here from a planetary theory fitted to NASA/JPL's DE421 ephemeris. What those positions mean is a matter of tradition — a 2,000-year-old symbolic system that this site describes, explains and takes seriously as cultural history. It is not treated here as a predictive science, and nothing on this site will tell you what is going to happen.

The two kinds of statement

Facts Positions, angular separations, retrograde motion, house cusps, precession, time zone conversion. All of it is arithmetic on an ephemeris and can be checked against any other astronomical source.
Tradition That Mars means drive, that a square is friction, that the 7th house is partnership. These are conventions of a symbolic system, reported as what the tradition holds — not as claims about how the world works.

How the positions are computed

Your local date, time and place are converted to a UTC instant using the IANA time zone database, then to a Julian Day, then corrected to Terrestrial Time with a ΔT polynomial. Planetary positions come from a truncated series fitted to JPL's DE421, with light-time correction iterated three times, annual aberration, nutation and precession applied. The Moon uses a separate lunar series. The Ascendant and Midheaven are derived from local sidereal time and the obliquity of date.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere to send it — this site has no backend, no analytics and no network calls of any kind once loaded.
